Product Philosophy & Safety Principles

AutoAssist AI is designed to reduce the technical information gap between vehicle owners and repair shops. By combining verified OEM technical manuals with accessible explanations and transparent repair benchmarks, we empower drivers to make informed, confident maintenance decisions.

  • AutoAssist AI is an informational tool and does NOT replace hands-on inspection by certified automotive professionals.
  • Estimated repair prices are based on Indian automotive aftermarket benchmarks in INR (₹) and vary by workshop, city, and vehicle trim.
  • When verified pricing data is not available in our records, we state "Pricing data unavailable" rather than fabricating estimates.
  • Critical vehicle safety hazards (e.g., brake hydraulic collapse, fuel leaks, loss of steering) always trigger an immediate DO NOT DRIVE recommendation.
